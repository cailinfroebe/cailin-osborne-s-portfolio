THE HANDS BEHIND THE WORK

A little
about me.

Hi, I’m Cailin! I’m an artist and designer studying design at BYU. I like making things that feel warm, colorful, and a little playful.

I don’t stick to just one medium. Sometimes it’s watercolor and cut paper, sometimes it’s a painting, a little animation, or a wooden character you can actually play with. I like getting to try a bit of everything.

This site is a collection of things I’ve made along the way—class projects, experiments, and things I made just for fun. Thanks for taking a look around. I’m happy you’re here!
